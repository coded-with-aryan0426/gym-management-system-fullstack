ANIMATION EASING FUNCTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ELASTIC SPRING: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
  Overshoot: 56% (playful, bouncy)
  Used for: Bars, bouncing elements
  Effect: Spring-like bounce with return

SMOOTH EASE: cubic-bezier(0.42, 0, 0.58, 1)
  Overshoot: None (smooth, professional)
  Used for: Line drawing, reveals
  Effect: Natural deceleration

BOUNCE: cubic-bezier(0.68, -0.55, 0.265, 1.55)
  Overshoot: Multiple (heartbeat-like)
  Used for: Heart, pulse effects
  Effect: Oscillating rhythm

STAGGER TIMING
  50ms: Barely noticeable
  100ms: Clear sequence
  150ms: Strong cascade
  300ms: Creates rhythm

  Base: 1.5px → 1.8-2px (refined)

  Bottom/end: 0.3-0.6 (subtle)
  Highlights: 0.2-0.3 (very subtle)

SHADOWS (feGaussianBlur)
  Blur: 1-1.5px

ANIMATIONS

  Target: 60fps sustained
